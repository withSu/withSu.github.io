research interests

My main interests are self-improvement and self-evolution: AI systems that learn from their own experience and carry useful feedback into the next iteration.

Generate candidates, evaluate them, and use feedback to improve the next iteration.

MLLM self-improvement & self-evolution

How can multimodal language models use feedback to improve their own behavior? I am interested in making iterative improvement useful beyond a single interaction.

Multimodal learning

Learning from complementary information across modalities, and understanding how those signals can support more capable AI systems.

Industrial anomaly detection

Recognizing anomalous objects in industrial settings, informed by my undergraduate work on semiconductor microdevices.
