I am an integrated M.S./Ph.D. student at Hanyang University and a member of the Intelligent Mobile Computing Lab (IMC Lab).

My research focuses on self-improving and self-evolving AI, with particular interests in:

  1. MLLM Self-Improvement: Learning from feedback to improve multimodal language models.
  2. Multimodal Learning: Learning from complementary information across modalities.
  3. Industrial Anomaly Detection: Detecting anomalous objects in industrial settings.

I joined IMC Lab in January 2026 and began the integrated M.S./Ph.D. program in September 2026. Previously, I conducted undergraduate research on semiconductor microdevice anomaly detection at CBNU Robotics Lab.
